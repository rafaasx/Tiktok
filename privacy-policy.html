Privacy Policy — ASR Publisher

Last updated: 18 July 2026

ASR Publisher ("the App") is a personal, single-user automation tool operated by Rafael Xavier ("the Operator") to publish the Operator's own video content to the Operator's own social media accounts, including TikTok, via each platform's official API. The App is not a public service and is not offered to third parties.

1. What the App does

The App uploads video files created by the Operator to the Operator's own connected accounts. It authenticates to TikTok using OAuth 2.0 and uses the video.upload and video.publish scopes solely to post the Operator's own content to the Operator's own account.

2. Data we access and store

  • OAuth tokens (access token and refresh token) issued by TikTok for the Operator's own account. These are stored locally on the Operator's computer and are used only to publish content on the Operator's behalf.
  • The Operator's own video files and captions, read from local storage to be uploaded.

The App does not collect, process, or store data from any other users. It has no end users other than the Operator.

3. Data we do NOT collect

The App does not collect personal information from viewers, followers, or any third party. It does not track behavior, does not use analytics, and does not sell or share any data. It does not read other people's content.

4. Data sharing

No data is shared with any third party. Tokens and content are transmitted only to the official TikTok API for the purpose of publishing the Operator's own posts.

5. Data retention and deletion

Tokens are stored locally and can be revoked by the Operator at any time from the TikTok account settings, or deleted by removing the local token file. Uploaded videos live on the Operator's own TikTok account and are managed there.

6. Security

Credentials are stored in a local, access-restricted directory and are never exposed publicly or committed to version control.

7. Contact

For any question about this policy, contact the Operator at rafaeltwisted@gmail.com.
